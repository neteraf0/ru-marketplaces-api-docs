## Endpoints

- [`GET` /api/v3/dbs/orders/new](GET__api_v3_dbs_orders_new.md) — Get New Orders List
- [`GET` /api/v3/dbs/orders](GET__api_v3_dbs_orders.md) — Get Information on Completed Orders
- [`POST` /api/v3/dbs/groups/info](POST__api_v3_dbs_groups_info.md) — Get Information on Paid Delivery
- [`POST` /api/v3/dbs/orders/client](POST__api_v3_dbs_orders_client.md) — Buyer Information
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/b2b/info](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_b2b_info.md) — B2B Buyer Information
- [`POST` /api/v3/dbs/orders/delivery-date](POST__api_v3_dbs_orders_delivery_date.md) — Delivery Date and Time
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/status/info](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_status_info.md) — Get Assembly Order Statuses
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/status/cancel](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_status_cancel.md) — Cancel Assembly Orders
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/status/confirm](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_status_confirm.md) — Transfer to Assembly
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/stickers](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_stickers.md) — Get Stickers for Assembly Orders with Delivery to Pickup Point
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/status/deliver](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_status_deliver.md) — Transfer to Delivery
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/status/receive](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_status_receive.md) — Notify that the Orders Are Received
- [`POST` /api/marketplace/v3/dbs/orders/status/reject](POST__api_marketplace_v3_dbs_orders_status_reject.md) — Notify that the Orders Are Declined
