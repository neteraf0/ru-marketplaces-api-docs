## Endpoints

- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/shipments/reception-transfer-act](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__shipments_reception_transfer_act.md) — Подтверждение ближайшей отгрузки и получение акта приема-передачи для нее
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId.md) — Получение информации об одной отгрузке
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/orders/info](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__orders_info.md) — Получение информации о возможности печати ярлыков
- [`POST` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/confirm](POST__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__confirm.md) — Подтверждение отгрузки
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/act](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__act.md) — Получение акта приема-передачи
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/inbound-act](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__inbound_act.md) — Получение фактического акта приема-передачи
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/transportation-waybill](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__transportation_.md) — Получение транспортной накладной
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/discrepancy-act](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__discrepancy_act.md) — Получение акта расхождений
- [`PUT` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/pallets](PUT__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__pallets.md) — Передача количества упаковок для доверительной приемки
- [`GET` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/pallet/labels](GET__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__pallet_labels.md) — Ярлыки для доверительной приемки
- [`PUT` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments](PUT__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments.md) — Получение информации о нескольких отгрузках
- [`POST` /v2/campaigns/{campaignId}/first-mile/shipments/{shipmentId}/orders/transfer](POST__v2_campaigns__campaignId__first_mile_shipments__shipmentId__orders_transfe.md) — Перенос заказов в следующую отгрузку
